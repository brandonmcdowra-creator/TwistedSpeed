TWISTED SPEED

Director review dashboard · rev-1 · browser vertical slice

1. What I built & verified

Twisted Speed is a top-down combat racer: drive the Balanced Brawler, fire front MGs and rear rockets, dodge a living track (electrified dunes that chase, spike walls that re-home, collapsing bridges), fight 3 rival AI cars, bank scrap, and spend it on Speed / Armor / Firepower. Progress is a Night Run ladder toward freedom from the dystopian overlords (3 nights → freedom ending). Placeholder art; synth SFX.

60-second playtest

  1. Open web/index.html (or serve web/).
  2. ENTER → garage → ENTER to launch Night 1.
  3. WASD drive the loop; J guns; K rockets; grab orange scrap; avoid cyan dunes / spikes.
  4. Past ~70% of the lap the track goes HOSTILE — finish or wreck.
  5. On results: press 1/2/3 to buy up to 2 upgrades, ENTER back to garage.

Build check: PASSED — see BUILD-CHECK.md. Boots clean (favicon 404 only), scripts load, player+rivals+hazards visible, input works, win/lose loop closes.

3. Needs your call

Q1 — How many nights to freedom?

V1 uses 3 short night runs on one track with escalating aggression.

Default if blank: keep 3 nights.

Q2 — Race length feel?

One lap of a large oval (~15–30s if driven clean). Good for “one more night,” short vs 15–30 min pitch.

Default: keep 1 lap; add length later via multi-lap nights.

Q3 — Second vehicle when?

V1 ships only the Brawler. Speedster / Crusher are in the pitch but cut for vertical slice.

Default: add Speedster after core fun is confirmed.

Q4 — Rockets: rear-only or also forward?

Spec said rear rocket pods; rockets fire backward with mild homing.

Default: keep rear pods (identity vs pure forward shooters).

Q5 — Difficulty of living track

Hostile phase at 72% can feel sudden. Dunes deal DoT while overlapping.

Default: keep hostile spike; telegraph earlier if you find it unfair.
